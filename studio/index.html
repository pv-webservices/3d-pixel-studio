Operational Integrity

Confidential project handling

Project drawings, BIM/CAD data, references and production files are handled within a studio-controlled workflow, with access limited to the people directly involved in the project.

File sharing and final delivery are managed through agreed transfer methods, including client-preferred channels where required. Project information is not shared outside the production team without authorization.
